---
title: What's coming in Numax v0.1.3
description: A small hardening release before moving on to bigger things.
---

`v0.1.2` was about performance and profiling, before moving on to bigger features, `v0.1.3` takes a small but important step: making Numax a little safer and harder to break.

The focus is supply-chain checks and fuzzing. That means better control over dependencies and CI workflows, verifiable release artifacts, and more aggressive testing of the network protocol and CRDTs with malformed inputs and unexpected operation sequences.

There are no big new runtime features here, and that is intentional. 
This is a short hardening release: strengthen the foundations, fix what the new checks find, and then move full speed into management APIs, peer discovery, and the rest of the roadmap.

Let's say nothing flashy, but absolutely vital before putting more irons in the fire.

**Stay tuned.** TY!!!
